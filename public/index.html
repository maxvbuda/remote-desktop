Remote Desktop

Reach your Mac from anywhere

Share your screen or take full control of another Mac. Connections go directly between devices, and you approve every one.

Download for Mac

macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Free

Share in one click

Get an ID and PIN to give to whoever is connecting. You see where they are and accept or decline.

Full control

Mouse, keyboard, scrolling and ⌘ shortcuts. Control stays off until you switch it on.

iPhone & iPad

View and control a Mac by touch: tap to click, hold to right-click, two fingers to scroll.

Getting started

  1. Download and unzip, then drag Remote Desktop into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. On the Mac you want to reach, click Start sharing and allow Screen Recording when asked.
  4. On the other Mac, open the Connect tab and enter the ID and PIN.
  5. To let them control the Mac, turn on Allow viewers to control this Mac and allow Accessibility when asked.

iPhone and iPad

The iOS app isn't on the App Store yet. To install it now, build RemoteViewer from the source code in Xcode with your Apple ID.
